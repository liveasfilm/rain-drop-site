Privacy policy

This policy covers RAIN DROP, operated by LIVEASFILM and distributed on Google Play through Eunsuminsung Studio. Contact for access, correction or deletion: liveasfilm@gmail.com.

Information and purposes

  • On your device: progress, free skins, settings, record link code and ad cadence.
  • Rankings and linking: an anonymous identifier, nickname, verified lowest altitude and period records. Nicknames, altitudes and ranks are public. Input replays are processed in memory during validation.
  • Online races: room membership, positions, inputs and results to run the match. Network IP addresses support connections and abuse prevention.
  • Purchases and rewards: product and transaction identifiers, account association, verification status/time and, when enabled, ad reward and admission history for restoration and duplicate prevention. The app does not collect payment card numbers.
  • Advertising SDK: Google AdMob in mobile apps may process IP and approximate location, device/ad identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Support: your reply address and information you choose to include in email.

Providers and transfers

Servers run on Vultr in Seoul. Apple/Google verify purchases and Google AdMob provides advertising. Providers may process data on their global infrastructure. Service connections use HTTPS/WSS. We do not offer email/password registration or collect contacts, microphone recordings or precise location.

Choices and retention

Offline play does not require a server. Ranking submission, online races and purchases use a connection. Where consent is required, use the consent form and available advertising privacy options to change your choices. Device settings let you manage advertising identifiers.

Linked records and restoration information are kept while needed to provide the service. Verified deletion requests are normally handled within 30 days. Minimal transaction information may be retained as required for legal obligations, refund disputes or fraud prevention; any applicable exception will be explained. Restricted recovery backups rotate, and completed deletion requests must be reapplied after a restore.

Access, correction and deletion

Use Support & privacy on the home screen or our data deletion page. Store refunds are a separate process. Material changes are reflected in this page and its update date.

Intended audience

This game is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us above if you believe a child has provided personal information.
